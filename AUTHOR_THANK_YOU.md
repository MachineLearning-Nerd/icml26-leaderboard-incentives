# Thank you to the authors

Thank you to Yatong Chen, Guanhua Zhang, and Moritz Hardt for making the
paper and its source available. The clear game-theoretic framing made it
possible to turn broad ranking and tune-before-test claims into explicit
contracts, exact counterexamples, controls, and reproducible evidence.

The audit is maintained by
[MachineLearning-Nerd](https://github.com/MachineLearning-Nerd). It is
